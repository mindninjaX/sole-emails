The problem

AI won't fix a broken operating model.

01

Stack sprawl — 8–12 tools, no unified view.

CRM, workflow, KYC, scheduling, comms — your team stitches truth together instead of doing the work.
02

Margin pressure — you find out when it's already late.

Jobs drift overdue. Reviews stack up. Billing slips. By the time you spot the bottleneck, margin's already gone.
03

AI risk — AI on top of silos is just more silos.

Sole puts AI inside the workflow — it sees what's missing, what's overdue, who's overloaded. One brain, not ten.
04

Client control — you don't own the client layer.

If clients log in to someone else's app, the relationship belongs to them. Sole runs firm-owned onboarding, comms and payments — branded as you.
05

Integrations — plays nicely with what you already run.

Native Xero, MYOB, ASIC, ATO and bank feeds. Keep the ledger you trust — Sole runs the operating layer around it.
