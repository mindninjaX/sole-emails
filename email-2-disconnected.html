What SPM does about it

One place. Not another login.

SPM is built to connect the work, not add another disconnected tool. It gives your firm one place to manage clients, onboarding, jobs, staff capacity, billing, collections and the client portal.

Fewer subscriptions. Less double-handling. A clearer view of what is moving, what is stuck, and what needs attention.

AI inside the workflow

AI sits inside the workflow, flagging what is missing, what is overdue and what needs attention next. It is not a separate app to log into.

You can use SPM under the Sole brand, or offer it under your own.
